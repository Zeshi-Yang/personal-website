Research Projects in Additive Manufacturing

My Ph.D. work centred on advancing process control and material performance across several additive-manufacturing platforms. Contributions include
• Binder–powder interaction studies in binder jetting
• Uniform powder spreading techniques
• Laser-parameter optimisation and spatter mitigation for laser powder-bed fusion
• Process tuning in filament-deposition modelling
• Quality upgrades for additively produced injection-mould tooling

To support real-time quality assurance, I have also implemented acoustic-emission sensing for in-situ monitoring, capturing process dynamics at production speed.

As a final-year project tutor I supervise undergraduate capstones, strengthening students’ experimental design, data analysis, and technical-communication skills.

These combined experiences give me a comprehensive perspective on metal additive manufacturing—from fundamental process physics to shop-floor deployment. I welcome collaboration with researchers and industrial partners aiming to scale these technologies.
