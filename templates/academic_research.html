Research Projects in Additive Manufacturing

My Ph.D. work centred on process control and material performance across additive-manufacturing platforms. I use that background to assess manufacturability, technical risk, and scale-up readiness in industrial technologies.

Process platforms Additive-manufacturing process development

Contributions include binder jetting, powder spreading, laser powder-bed fusion, filament-deposition modelling, and injection-mould tooling.

Monitoring / quality assurance Acoustic-emission sensing

Built in-situ monitoring workflows that capture process dynamics at production speed, supporting real-time quality assurance and defect detection.

Process optimisation Spatter mitigation and parameter tuning

Focused on parameter windows, defect mechanisms, and process robustness rather than isolated lab demonstrations.

Technical leadership Final-year project tutoring

Supervise undergraduate capstones and strengthen experimental design, data analysis, and technical communication.

These projects cover metal additive manufacturing from process physics to shop-floor deployment. I welcome collaboration with researchers, operators, and industrial partners working to scale these technologies.
