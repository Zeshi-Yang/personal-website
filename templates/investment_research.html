Welcome to my investment research projects portfolio!

These projects were initially developed as personal experiments during my studies, and have since evolved into applied tools shaped by real-world investment workflows during my internship. My most recent work includes the development of an advanced stock screening system, combining financial metric weighting, sector-specific adjustments, and automated ranking logic. This project integrates data from Refinitiv and outputs dynamic reports, helping prioritize equities for fundamental analysis. I’ve also built a news aggregation engine that leverages keyword filtering, sentiment analysis, and LLM-based summarization to extract actionable insights from financial news. This tool enhances real-time awareness around portfolio-relevant companies. Another highlight is the macro dashboard, designed using Python Dash and Refinitiv data. It visualizes key macroeconomic indicators to guide top-down investment decisions, with interactive filters and country/time-based views for flexibility. Previously, I created a trading signal framework—my first attempt at applying programming to market logic. Though initially hobby-driven, it laid the foundation for my ongoing research into systematic investing. Earlier personal projects include web scraping scripts to collect structured data across online sources, and an account tracking program for secure personal finance monitoring. These projects represent a shift from hobbyist coding to real-world research tools, blending my passion for finance, data, and automation. They continue to strengthen my adaptability and deepen my understanding of how technology supports modern investment analysis.
