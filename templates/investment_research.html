Investment Research Systems & Automation

Developed across research, investing, and workflow-automation contexts, these systems are designed to support professional sourcing, diligence, monitoring, and decision support.

Sourcing / screening Multi-Factor Stock Screener

Integrates Refinitiv fundamentals with sector-specific factor weightings and automated ranking logic to produce dynamic shortlists for deeper fundamental work.

Company analysis / diligence Agentic Company-Analysis Workflow

Converts filings, reports, and transcripts into structured Markdown and agent-assisted diligence outputs, including summarisation, thematic synthesis, and investor-grade Q&A.

Monitoring / intelligence Real-Time News Intelligence Engine

Combines sentiment scoring and LLM-assisted ranking to surface the highest-signal headlines for portfolio and watchlist names.

Macro / market context Interactive Macro Dashboard

Built in Python Dash to visualise macro indicators with country and time-series filters, helping frame allocation decisions with current context.

Systematic experimentation Systematic Trading-Signal Framework

Research sandbox for rule-based alpha generation, signal evaluation, and execution-logic testing.

Supporting infrastructure Supporting Utilities

Custom web-scraping pipelines and a secure account-tracking module that automate data collection and personal P&L monitoring.
